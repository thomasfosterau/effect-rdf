New package `@thomasfosterau/effect-xmp`: XMP (ISO 16684-1) packets as RDF — packet scanning, unwrapping and padding; the XMP data model over a `Dataset` (simple values, structures, `rdf:Bag` / `rdf:Seq` / `rdf:Alt` arrays, qualifiers); a serializer for the canonical XMP form; typed accessors for Dublin Core, XMP Basic, XMP Rights, XMP Media Management, PDF, PDF/UA and PDF/A (identification and extension schemas); and XMP extraction from JPEG (with Extended XMP) and PNG files. `@thomasfosterau/effect-rdfxml` gains `RdfXml.parseDocument`, which reads RDF/XML from an already-parsed `Xml.Document`.

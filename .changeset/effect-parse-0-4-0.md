Require `@thomasfosterau/effect-parse` `^0.4.0` (was `^0.2.0`), the version the grammars are built and tested against. On 0.x a caret range admits only its own minor line, so `^0.2.0` would have refused 0.4. A consumer must upgrade its own `@thomasfosterau/effect-parse` along with these packages.
